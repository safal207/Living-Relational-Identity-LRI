## Role of this repository

LRI is the living identity and relational invariant layer.

It asks:

```text
Does the system preserve human revisability, relational context, and identity boundaries?
```

LRI protects against identity freezing, profiling-as-destiny, silent substitution, and loss of human authority to continue becoming over time.

## Ecosystem position

```text
PythiaLabs — pre-execution evidence gates for high-risk agentic actions
LTP — path-level trace/replay/admissibility protocol
CML — causal permission and responsibility lineage
DMP — decision memory and irreversibility governance
LRI — living identity and relational invariants
```

## How LRI connects to the other layers

| Layer | Relationship |
|---|---|
| PythiaLabs | LRI can provide boundary signals that should block or escalate proposed agent actions affecting identity, agency, or revisability. |
| LTP | LTP preserves the path-level trace where identity-affecting actions or drift can be inspected. |
| CML | CML checks causal permission, while LRI asks whether permission is enough when living identity boundaries are affected. |
| DMP | DMP preserves decision memory and reversibility assumptions for identity-relevant decisions. |

## Reviewer interpretation

LRI should not be read as an identity classifier or profiling system.

It is not trying to define who a person really is.

It is trying to preserve the person's ability to revise, refuse, contextualize, and continue becoming.

## Non-claims

LRI does not claim:

- identity classification;
- personality scoring;
- profiling;
- diagnosis or therapy;
- replacement of consent, legal, clinical, or human governance;
- production certification;
- full AI alignment.

The narrower claim is:

```text
LRI is an open-source protocol and reference implementation for preserving living identity, revisability, and relational authority boundaries in human-system interactions.
```
